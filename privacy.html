Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Publisher ("the Application") handles data. The Application is a personal, non-commercial tool used by its owner to publish videos to their own authorized social media account.

2. Data We Access

To publish content, the Application uses access tokens provided by the social media platform through its official authentication flow. These tokens are stored securely on a private server and are used only to publish content to the authorizing account.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

The Application does not collect personal information from visitors of this website. It does not use cookies, analytics, or trackers. It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Access tokens are retained only as long as necessary to operate the Application and can be revoked at any time by the account owner through their platform settings.

5. Third-Party Platforms

The Application interacts with third-party platforms through their official APIs. Use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies.
